\section{Extended Methods and Study Progression}

The main paper is organized around the story of what we learned, rather than the chronology in which we learned it. This appendix records the study progression explicitly, so that a reader who wants to see how the final evidence set was built can trace each stage to the lesson it contributed.

The implementation of the selector evolved over the course of the study, but the reporting discipline stayed constant: selector revisions were admitted only when motivated by a clearly identified failure mode, reportable profiling was kept separate from diagnostic profiling, canonical evidence bundles were regenerated after major stages, and the final paper bundle and claim inventory were kept in sync before the manuscript was frozen.
