The implementation changed along the way, but the paper-facing method remained disciplined:
\begin{itemize}
\item selector revisions were admitted only when motivated by observed failure modes;
\item reportable and diagnostic profiling were kept separate;
\item canonical artifact bundles were regenerated after major rounds;
\item and final claims were locked only after the paper bundle and final claim inventory agreed on their sources.
\end{itemize}
