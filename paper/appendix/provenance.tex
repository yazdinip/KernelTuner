\section{Provenance and Canonical Sources}

The final manuscript is tied to a frozen evidence snapshot. This appendix records the reporting boundary explicitly, so that a reader or reviewer can tell, without guessing, which study outputs contributed to the paper and which ones did not.

\paragraph{Canonical sources.} All claims and figures in the main text are drawn from repository-local, completed, and canonical evidence. The final claim wording comes from the project's final claim inventory, the figure and table sources come from the frozen final-paper evidence bundle, and any historical interpretation we use in the narrative is cross-referenced against dated synthesis logs preserved in the project repository.

\paragraph{Explicit exclusions.} The draft deliberately does \emph{not} draw on evidence from incomplete or superseded study campaigns, scratch-only experiment directories, raw runs that were moved outside the tracked evidence bundle, or optional aligned-refresh and confirmation reruns that were intentionally skipped for budget reasons. These exclusions are part of the paper's reporting discipline.

\paragraph{Why the final claim set is narrower than the project history.} The project explored substantially more configurations, selector revisions, and diagnostic paths than appear in the final main text. That narrowing is intentional: the paper is not meant to re-narrate every operational step, but to present the strongest bounded story supported by the canonical repository-local evidence. The dated logs remain the right place to preserve the full chronology and iteration history for handoff and for future follow-up work.
